# GVD Process Map: How a Global Value Dossier Gets Built

An interactive map of the end-to-end process for creating a Global Value Dossier: every evidence module, the team responsible for it, the hand-offs between them, and the health technology assessment (HTA) bodies and payers the dossier is ultimately written for.

**[Open the interactive map](https://gvd-processflow.netlify.app/)** · [Source](gvd-process-map.html) (single HTML file; download it and open in a browser)

## Why this exists

A **Global Value Dossier (GVD)** is the master document a drug manufacturer assembles to prove a product's clinical, humanistic and economic value. Country teams then adapt it into submissions for HTA bodies, the agencies that decide whether a drug is worth paying for, such as NICE in England, HAS in France and IQWiG in Germany.

Building one draws on around a dozen functions, including HEOR, biostatistics, clinical development, medical affairs, regulatory, pricing, legal and country market access. It takes months, and no one person usually sees the whole chain. This map is the first step towards a product that shortens the GVD timeline. It shows where evidence comes from, who owns each step, and where the bottlenecks are, such as multi-cycle Medical-Legal-Regulatory (MLR) review.

## The five phases

| Phase | What happens | Key modules |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Upstream evidence | Gather everything published and generated about the drug and the disease | Systematic literature review (SLR), targeted literature review (TLR), clinical trial data, epidemiology and burden of disease |
| 2. Evidence generation | Turn raw evidence into comparisons payers will accept | Network meta-analysis / indirect treatment comparison, patient-reported outcomes and quality of life, real-world evidence, regulatory label review |
| 3. HTA strategy and economics | Model value and plan country submissions | Health economic model (cost-effectiveness, budget impact), HTA dossier strategy, value story, pricing and access strategy |
| 4. Dossier assembly | Write the four core GVD modules | Disease background, clinical evidence, economic value, references and evidence base |
| 5. Review and adaptation | Approve and localise | MLR review, external scientific review (KOLs, advisory boards), country and payer adaptation |

Click any module on the map to see its inputs, outputs, data sources and owning team.

## Terms

- **HEOR**: Health Economics and Outcomes Research, the function that quantifies a drug's value in cost and outcomes terms.
- **SLR / TLR**: a systematic literature review follows a strict, reproducible protocol (PRISMA). A targeted review is faster and answers a narrower question.
- **NMA / ITC**: network meta-analysis and indirect treatment comparison, statistical methods for comparing drugs that were never tested head-to-head.
- **ICER and cost per QALY**: the incremental cost-effectiveness ratio, which is the extra cost per extra unit of health gained. A QALY (quality-adjusted life year) is one year of life in perfect health.
- **KOL**: key opinion leader, a recognised clinical expert.
