Life sciences product concepts

Four working prototypes for pharma and biotech commercial teams

Each one starts from a real commercial workflow problem and goes from problem statement and requirements to a prototype you can use. Everything below runs in your browser on synthetic data.

Cortex

Pricing, contracting and market access analysts

Governed AI analytics: ask a question in plain English and get an answer built from certified data, with the SQL and sources shown.

  • Semantic layer turns questions into SQL that runs in the browser
  • Gross-to-net waterfalls, rebate exposure, renewal and exception-clause queries
  • Restricted datasets are blocked; upload a CSV; optional LLM
Open the analyst workspaceDocs and source

ClearAuth

Providers, patients and payers

Prior authorization in one place, with a coverage-policy engine that checks each request against the plan's criteria and explains the gaps.

  • Provider, patient and payer views over the same cases
  • Criteria checks with evidence, fixes and a fit score; appeal and determination drafts
  • Demo runs in the browser; Docker or Render for the full server; optional LLM
Open ClearAuthDocs and source

Pharma Action Builder

Field teams, commercial analytics and commercial excellence

Next Best Action for HCP engagement, plus a no-code workbench that trains and evaluates real models.

  • Gradient boosting, random forest, logistic regression and CART, trained in the browser
  • Held-out AUC, confusion matrix and feature importance
  • Per-HCP best tactic by expected value, compared with a one-channel policy
Open Action BuilderDocs and source

GVD Process Map

Market access, HEOR and medical affairs

The end-to-end process for building a Global Value Dossier: every evidence module, its owner, the hand-offs and the HTA bodies it serves.

  • Five phases from literature review to country adaptation
  • Click any module for inputs, outputs, data sources and owning team
Open the mapDocs and source

Run your own copy

Fork the repository, then go to Settings → Actions and enable workflows, and Settings → Pages and choose GitHub Actions as the source. The next push publishes all four prototypes at your own address. Other options (Netlify, Vercel, Docker, Render, a local LLM) are in the deployment guide.
